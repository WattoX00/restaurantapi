How to Use

  • Menu – Manage menu items. Add foods, update availability, or remove items.
  • Orders – Create and manage customer orders by table number.
  • Statistics – View sales insights like most sold items, least sold items, and monthly revenue.
  • Login is required for protected actions like editing or updating data.
  • Use the navigation buttons above to move between modules.
